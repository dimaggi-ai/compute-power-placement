# References

Claims trace to the sources below, verified against primary sources in August 2026. Prices and volumes move; the model exposes every calibration value as an input to re-point.

- **[1]** US ISO 5-minute nodal locational marginal prices (the literal heat map): ERCOT live LMP contour map (ercot.com/mktinfo/prices); EIA Wholesale Electricity Market Portal aggregating all seven ISO/RTOs (eia.gov/electricity/wholesalemarkets); GridStatus.io live nodal maps. Dispersion: CAISO ~13% of 2024 hours at negative prices (median ≈ −$17/MWh); one ERCOT node averaged $28,187/MWh for an hour, Feb 19 2025 — nodal LMP = energy + congestion + loss, and only the energy component is capped, so nodal prices legitimately exceed any system-wide cap. ERCOT's RTC+B go-live (2025-12-05) split the former single $5,000/MWh system-wide offer cap into $5,000 day-ahead and **$2,000 real-time**.
- **[2]** Curtailment: ~20 TWh US wind+solar curtailed in 2024 (Amperon, 2025); CAISO 3.4 TWh (93% solar, EIA); ERCOT >8 TWh (~1.2 GW continuous). US data-center electricity 176 TWh in 2023 (LBNL 2024 Data Center Energy Report) — so curtailed renewables alone ≈ 11% of total DC load.
- **[3]** Interconnection/capacity: ERCOT large-load queue ~226 GW (Nov 2025, ~77% data centers); PJM capacity price record $329.17/MW-day (2026/27 auction), driven by data-center load (IEEE Spectrum / IEEFA; utilitydive).
- **[4]** GPU-hour vs energy cost: a burdened H100 draws ~1.2–1.4 kW incl. cooling; at $60/MWh that is ~$0.072–0.084/h against a ~$2–3 GPU-hour on-demand price — energy ≈ 3–4% of cost. **This 3–4% band is derived here from those inputs; no source publishes it.** Independent TCO analyses put energy at ~7–11% because they assume $83–120/MWh (ITK Services; Epoch AI). The conclusion (energy is a minority of the GPU-hour) holds across the whole range; the percentage does not. (Public cloud GPU pricing; NVIDIA H100 power specs.)
- **[5]** Training-state size and migration: full state ≈ 16 bytes/param (fp16 params 2 + fp16 grads 2 + fp32 params/momentum/variance 4+4+4, ZeRO convention, Rajbhandari et al., arXiv:1910.02054) → ~6.5 TB for 405B; measured checkpoint-load median ~33 min on a 504-GPU cluster (arXiv:2605.09370); GPU CRIU checkpoint/restore on NVIDIA since early 2024. WAN egress $0.02–0.09/GB (cloud egress schedules).
- **[6]** Grid-interactive headroom: Duke Nicholas Institute, "Rethinking Load Growth" (Feb 2025) — 76 GW of new load integrable with existing capacity if new loads curtail **0.25% of annual ENERGY** — for a 100 GW load, 2,190 GWh of 876,000 GWh (House testimony, Norris, Mar 2025, fn. 12). Not 0.25% of hours: this repo previously read it that way. Duke also reports 98 GW at 0.5% and 126 GW at 1.0%, so 0.5% is not a cliff.
- **[7]** Inference as relocatable load: "AI Inference as Relocatable Electricity Demand" (arXiv:2604.27855); CWind/XWind — "890+ GW of wind capacity lies within 50 ms network RTT of Azure data centers" (arXiv:2605.23348).
- **[8]** Synchronous multi-DC training limits: Gemini technical report (arXiv:2312.11805) — SuperPods combined across datacenters, synchronous training works because "Google's network latencies and bandwidths are sufficient"; SemiAnalysis, "Multi-Datacenter Training" (Sep 2024) — 43.2 ms coast-to-coast RTT kills naive synchronous training; 5 Pbit/s inter-campus moves 400 TB of weights in 0.64 s.
- **[9]** Low-communication distributed training: DiLoCo (DeepMind, arXiv:2311.08105) — 500× less communication at matched quality; Streaming DiLoCo (arXiv:2501.18512) — 400× total-bit reduction; Prime Intellect INTELLECT-1 — 10B params trained across 3 continents / 30 providers. Proven to ~10B, not frontier scale.
- **[10]** Google spatial load shifting: "Carbon-aware computing… shift moveable compute tasks between different data centers, based on regional hourly carbon-free energy availability" (Google blog, May 2021), launched with media processing; carbon-intelligent capacity curves (arXiv:2106.11750).
- **[11]** EPRI DCFlex initiative (Oct 2024 → ~40 participants incl. Google, Meta, NVIDIA, ERCOT, PJM); demo list includes "Ashburn VA and Chicago for geospatial load shifting."
- **[12]** Power-seeking operators: Crusoe (flare-gas → gigawatt firm-power AI campuses; Stargate Abilene 1.2 GW); Lancium (Clean Campuses at renewable-congested nodes; Fort Stockton qualified as an ERCOT Controllable Load Resource, Nov 2022); Soluna (>112,000 MWh of curtailed wind consumed in 2024).
- **[13]** Grid-interactive datacenters: Emerald AI — Phoenix trial, 256 GPUs, 25% power reduction for 3 h without SLA violation (arXiv:2507.00909); NVIDIA + Emerald + EPRI + Digital Realty + PJM 96 MW Aurora AI Factory (Oct 2025), first power-flexibility-certified facility.
- **[14]** Geographical load-balancing lineage: Qureshi et al., "Cutting the Electric Bill for Internet-Scale Systems," SIGCOMM 2009 (price-differential request routing); Liu, Lin, Wierman, Low, Andrew, "Greening Geographical Load Balancing," SIGMETRICS 2011 (with the caveat that price-chasing can raise total energy). AI-era revival: "To Defer or To Shift?" (arXiv:2604.05376), PowerAtlas (arXiv:2607.26710).
